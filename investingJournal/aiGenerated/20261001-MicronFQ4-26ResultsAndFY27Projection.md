# Micron FQ4'26 results and FY27 projection

October 1st, 2026. Follows [[20260708-MicronMU2026Thesis]]. Sources: the [8-K press release](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/0000723125/000072312526000018/a2026q4ex991-pressrelease.htm) and [prepared remarks](https://investors.micron.com/files/doc_financials/2026/q4/Q4-FY26-Prepared-Remarks.pdf), Sep 30, 2026. Consensus and price: [stockanalysis.com](https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/mu/forecast/), Oct 1.

## FQ4'26 (reported)

| | FQ4'26 | FQ3'26 | FY26 |
|---|---|---|---|
| Revenue | $54.2B | $41.5B | $133.2B |
| Gross margin (non-GAAP) | 87.0% | 84.9% | 81.1% |
| EPS (non-GAAP) | $33.42 | $25.11 | $75.52 |
| Operating cash flow / FCF | $44.0B / $33.2B | $25.4B / $18.3B | $89.7B / $62.3B |

- Beat the ~$50B guide by about $4B. FQ4 had **14 weeks** (FY26 was a 53-week year), so growth over Q3 was +31% as reported but about +21% per week.
- DRAM was $39.8B (bits up mid-single digits, prices up high teens). NAND was $14.1B (bits +10%, prices +30%).
- Micron now has 26 strategic customer agreements (multi-year take-or-pay contracts). Remaining contracted revenue is about $150B, and customers have committed $32B, mostly cash deposits. Management says margins stay above any prior cycle peak even at contract floor prices.

## What Micron guided

- **FQ1'27:** revenue $61.5B ± $1.5B, gross margin 86.25%, EPS $38.15 ± $1.00 (non-GAAP).
- FQ1 is the **gross-margin floor for FY27**. Revenue grows every quarter, and prices keep rising but "more moderate[ly]."
- Opex rises about $2.5B in FY27. Tax rate about 15.5%, share count about 1.15B.
- Capex is about $11.5B in FQ1 and about $25B for the first half, higher in the second half. That puts FY27 at roughly **$55B, double FY26**. Most of the increase is construction for cleanrooms that come online in late CY28 and later.
- Industry DRAM bits grow mid-20s % in CY26 and low-20s % in CY27. Industry NAND grows mid-20s % in CY27. The industry stays supply-constrained through 2028.

## FY27 projection

Method: start from FQ1's guided cost of goods ($8.46B). Grow it with bit volume, with cash cost per bit falling about 1% a quarter. Add depreciation built from the capex schedule. Revenue in each quarter is set by the gross-margin path, which means each margin path is really a price path.

**Bits.** Base case: DRAM about 10.9 EB (+19% vs FY26) and NAND about 241 EB (+22%), roughly in line with industry. That's my assumption: Micron gave industry numbers for CY27 but none of its own. The only "below industry" statement was about NAND in CY26. If Micron grows below industry, use the low case (FY27 EPS about $3–5 lower). The range is +14% to +22% for DRAM. New fabs (ID1, Tongluo) start in mid-CY27 and add almost nothing to FY27.

**Margin paths** (FQ1 fixed at 86.25%):

| Path | FQ4 margin | Price change per quarter |
|---|---|---|
| Floor | 86.25% | 0% |
| Mid | 88% | +4–5% |
| High | 90% | +10–13% |

**FY27 revenue / EPS / FCF (non-GAAP, base bits, $55B capex):**

| | Floor | Mid | High |
|---|---|---|---|
| Revenue | $264B | $283B | $312B |
| EPS | $164 | $179 | $200 |
| FCF | $132B | $141B | $154B |

- **Full range:** revenue $258–317B, EPS $161–203. **Most likely: $283–312B and $179–200.** Across the grid, margin matters about 5× as much as bits.
- **Capex mostly hits cash, not earnings.** D&A rises from $9.5B to about $11.7B, costing only 0.1–0.3 points of margin. Capex between $52B and $60B moves EPS by less than $1 and FCF by about $7.5B. The earnings hit from capex comes in FY28–29, when the new buildings go into service.
- GAAP EPS is about $1.25 lower. I assumed no buybacks, but larger capital returns start Dec 9, 2026, which is EPS upside.

## FY28 extension (calendar Q4 2027 to Q3 2028)

Each case continues its FY27 trend. Bits keep growing at the same quarterly rate (base: DRAM +19%, NAND +22%), which matches Micron's industry outlook for CY28. Prices continue each case's FY27 exit rate: flat, +5% a quarter, or +10% a quarter. Gross margin is now an output. Capex is about $70B, D&A rises to about $16B, and opex grows another $2.5B.

| Base bits | FY28 revenue | Gross margin | EPS | FCF | P/E at $1,050 |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Floor (flat prices) | $316B | 86.2% | $200 | $166B | 5.3x |
| **Mid (+5% a quarter)** | **$411B** | **89.4%** | **$270** | **$231B** | **3.9x** |
| High (+10% a quarter) | $558B | 92.2% | $379 | $331B | 2.8x |

- Full range across bit cases: revenue $294–589B, EPS $185–401. Mid is the most likely case.
- The high case needs prices up about 46% year over year for a second straight year, so treat it as an upper bound.
- **The contract price ceilings are not disclosed.** About 15–25% of revenue is probably price-capped: the agreements cover more than 35% of revenue, 3/4 of that has a pricing framework, and most of those have bands or fixed prices. Modelling ceilings at a 85% or 88% margin (85% already binds today) gives FY28 EPS of $254–265 in the mid case (vs $270 uncapped) and $338–358 in the high case (vs $379). The capped share matters more than where the ceiling sits. FY27 moves by $0–8.
- **Buybacks start to matter.** Without them, cash would pile up to $370–560B. Spending 50% of FCF on buybacks at about 7x earnings takes the share count from 1.15B to about 1.05B, adding about $9–20 to FY28 EPS.
- Capex is still a cash story: $60–80B moves FY28 EPS by about $1.

## Valuation vs the Street

MU traded at about $1,050 on Oct 1, roughly flat after the print. FY27 is exactly the next four quarters, so FY27 EPS gives the forward P/E.

| | FY27 revenue | FY27 EPS | Forward P/E |
|---|---|---|---|
| Analyst consensus (43 analysts) | $250.5B | $161.49 | 6.5x |
| My floor (flat prices) | $258–268B | $161–167 | 6.3–6.5x |
| **My mid / high** | **$283–312B** | **$179–200** | **5.3–5.9x** |

- Consensus is **below my floor case on revenue** and level with it on EPS. FQ1 guidance × 4 already gives $246B / $153, so consensus assumes almost no growth after FQ1. That contradicts the guide's "sequential revenue growth each quarter" and "higher margins beyond FQ1."
- The consensus snapshot is probably from before the guide. Consensus had FQ1 at $57.2B against the $61.5B guide, so expect upward revisions.
- Even on consensus, about 6.5x forward earnings means the market is pricing FY27 as a cycle peak. The median price target of $1,545 is about 9x my mid-case EPS.
- The [[20260708-MicronMU2026Thesis]] target of $4,000 would be 20–22x FY27 EPS of $180–200.

## Notes

- Micron's industry DRAM outlook (mid-20s % in CY26, low-20s % in CY27) only matches the **loose** scenario of [[20260712-MemoryBitSupplyDemandModel]]. The central scenario has about 12% in both years, so the upstream supply line may be too conservative.
- Things to watch next quarter: whether FQ1 beats the guide again (FQ4 came in above the top of its guided range), how fast prices rise in FQ2, and the size of the buyback program.
